**Figure. 6 Public-panel distribution of the shared plasmid backbone and candidate
genes.** (a) Distribution of the 128,837-bp backbone among the 159-genome public
panel: 153 genomes have detectable BLASTN alignments to the plasmid and are
classified as near-identical (≥95% colinear-chain coverage and ≥99%
length-weighted identity), partial (≥30% coverage), fragment (≥5% coverage) or
trace (<5% coverage); the remaining six genomes — all Lactococcus controls
(F44, G423, G50, IL1403, IL6288, IO-1; identity verified against the panel
metadata) — have no detectable alignment and are not shown. (b) Co-occurrence of
the three candidate genes across all 159 panel genomes at the 70%/70% threshold
(P, enterocin P-like; I, candidate immunity factor; H, hiracin JM79-like); the
Fisher exact test in Section 3.5 uses the same 159-genome sample set. All counts
shown are computed from the input tables
(`results/tables/supplementary/TableS4_homology_thresholds/pl1_distribution.tsv`
and `gene_presence_matrix.tsv`) by
`scripts/manuscript_v9/fig6_panel_distribution.py`; none are hardcoded.
